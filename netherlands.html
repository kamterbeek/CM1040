The Netherlands

Internet & Society

The development of Internet infrastructure changed more than just how people accessed information. It transformed communication, work, education, shopping and everyday life in the Netherlands.

01

From dial-up to broadband

Faster connections

During the 1990s, many home Internet users relied on dial-up connections. The introduction and expansion of broadband made Internet access much faster and more convenient.

Always connected

Broadband changed the way people used the Internet. Instead of connecting only when they needed to go online, households could increasingly treat the Internet as an everyday utility.

02

From the home computer to the mobile Internet

Wi-Fi

Wireless networking made it possible for people to connect laptops, phones and other devices without being physically connected to a router.

Smartphones

The growth of smartphones expanded Internet access beyond the home and workplace. People could access websites, communication services and online applications while travelling or away from a traditional computer.

03

The Internet in everyday Dutch life

Communication

Email, messaging, social media and video communication changed how people stayed in contact with friends, family and colleagues.

Education

Schools, universities and students increasingly rely on online resources, digital learning environments and Internet-based communication.

Shopping

Online shopping allowed consumers to research, compare and purchase products without visiting a physical store.

Work

Internet connectivity made remote communication, collaboration and working from outside the traditional office increasingly practical.

Public services

Government and other organisations increasingly provide information and services through digital platforms.

Entertainment

Streaming, online video, gaming and social media have changed how people consume and share entertainment.

Looking back

From connecting to the Internet to living online

The history of the Internet in the Netherlands is not simply a history of faster connections. It is also a history of how Internet access became integrated into everyday life.
